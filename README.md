# Prop Firm Rules & Research

Independent, evidence-backed deep-research artefacts on proprietary trading firms.

## BrightFunded

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `BrightFunded_Complete_Interactive_Research_2026-10-11.html` | **Main deliverable.** One standalone, offline-capable interactive research dashboard (HTML + CSS + JS + embedded data). Just open it in a browser. |
| `brightfunded_research_data_2026-10-11.json` | Machine-readable research database (firm, entities, plans, prices, add-ons, 51 rules, coupons, timeline, reviews, company facts, unresolved items, 48 sources). |
| `brightfunded_rules_2026-10-11.csv` | The rule database as a flat CSV. |

Research date: **11 October 2026**. Nothing here is affiliated with or endorsed by BrightFunded.

### Verification labels used everywhere

* **Verified** - directly supported by a current official BrightFunded rule, help-centre or legal page.
* **Officially advertised** - stated on an official marketing page only.
* **Historical** - supported by an older document; may not apply to new accounts.
* **Unverified / conflicting** - insufficient or contradictory evidence.

No purchase was completed, no account was created and no payment information was submitted during this research, so **live checkout totals and coupon validity are not checkout-verified**.
