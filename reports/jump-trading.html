c13Method and limitations

How this report was produced

  • The official domain was taken from the supplied directory list, or discovered and then verified by matching the firm name plus prop-firm vocabulary on the homepage.
  • The homepage, its sitemap(s) and up to twelve keyword-ranked internal pages were fetched, plus help/support subdomains where they exist.
  • Page text was extracted locally and matched against a fixed rule vocabulary. Every match stores the exact sentence and its URL.
  • Trustpilot and PropFirmMatch were read through a public text-reader proxy. Reddit and other blocked sources were not used here.
  • No login, no account creation, no purchase, no payment data, and no attempt to bypass access controls.

Known limitations

  • Extraction is pattern-based. A percentage shown here is the figure the firm published on that page - it may belong to a different programme than the one you are considering.
  • JavaScript-only sites can hide most rules from a crawler, producing ‘not found’ where a rule does exist.
  • Marketing pages are not contracts. The Terms & Conditions govern, and they were not always retrievable.
  • No live checkout price, no coupon validation and no payout confirmation was performed.
  • Review-platform data is third-party and, for affiliate marketplaces, commercially incentivised.
