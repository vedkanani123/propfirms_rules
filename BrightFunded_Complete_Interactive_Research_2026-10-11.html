08 Drawdown & risk calculator

BrightFunded uses two different drawdown mechanisms — static (2-Step plans) and trailing / high-water-mark (1-Step and the Free $1K). The calculator changes behaviour with the plan you selected. Daily-loss floors are calculated the way BrightFunded documents them: end-of-day highest of balance or equity, minus (daily % × original challenge size).
